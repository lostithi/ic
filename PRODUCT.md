# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and brand owners who need a website, search presence, and positioning as one hire — not three disconnected vendors. They arrive when a template site, scattered SEO, or weak message is already failing them.

## Product Purpose

This is Spine Studio’s public site. It should make the studio’s offer clear and convert a visit into a project brief through the contact form (`/#contact`, `/start`). Success is a serious enquiry, not pageviews.

## Positioning

Web, SEO, and strategy built as one vertical system — “the backbone” — so the brand stands upright. Neighbors can sell a prettier site or a search package; this studio’s claim is structure first, then the brand.

## Operating Context

Visitors land on the one-pager, descend the spine metaphor through services / work / process, and either open a case or send a brief. Paid traffic can enter via `/start`. The studio is reached at `hello@spinestudio.uk`. Live domain: `https://spinestudio.uk`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: marketing site (home, work index, case studies, start/ads landing), contact intake, 3D descent as brand mechanism.
- Host: Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext. Local: `npm run dev` (Next.js 16).
- Contact: Formspree form. Do not invent a second intake path.
- Undecided: which listed case studies are real clients vs concept placeholders (owner will mark later). Until marked, do not treat named work as verified proof.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Spine Studio (short: Spine). Code: `[SP-01]`.
- Voice: structure, backbone, vertebra, descent — precise, not decorative.
- Binding identity the owner already locked: black/white xerox/ink world, Oswald + IBM Plex Mono, S-curve mark, 3D skull/spine. Init does not expand this into a visual system; later design work must not discard it without an explicit rebrand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real operating facts: brand name, domain, email, offer copy in `lib/brand.ts` and `lib/content.ts`.
- Work entries in `lib/work.ts` are a mixed set (some real, some concept). Do not invent testimonials, press, metrics, or client quotes. Do not present unmarked case studies as proven results.

## Product Principles

- Structure before decoration.
- One system for web, search, and message.
- Convert to a brief, not a brochure.
- Do not fabricate proof.
- Keep the spine metaphor as the product’s mechanism, not a garnish.
